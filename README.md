# KEM'S CREATION — PROJECT FOUNDATION

We are building a real business application for **Kem's Creation**, a women's fashion business based in Badagry, Lagos, Nigeria.

I have attached four planning documents:

* BUSINESS_SCENARIO.md

* PRD.md

* DESIGN_SYSTEM_UX_SPEC.md

* EVIDENCE_OF_TRUTH.md

Treat these documents as the project's source of truth.

## Instructions and priorities

1. Read and follow all four documents before implementing anything.

2. Do not invent business information, prices, policies, testimonials, reviews, addresses, customer records or brand assets.

3. Clearly distinguish demo data from verified business information.

4. Follow the design direction in DESIGN_SYSTEM_UX_SPEC.md. Avoid generic SaaS templates and generic AI-generated visual patterns.

5. Prioritize mobile-first usability, accessibility, visual consistency and a professional fashion-oriented experience.

6. Preserve the product principle: **Automate coordination, not craftsmanship.**

## Build scope: Stage 1 only

Establish the initial application foundation.

Build:

* A responsive public-facing application shell.

* A separate owner/admin application shell and dashboard route.

* Clear and coherent navigation for both experiences.

* The temporary visual identity, typography, color system and spacing rules.

* Reusable buttons, form controls, cards and appointment-status components where appropriate.

* A clean, maintainable page and component structure.

* Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop.

The public experience should feel editorial, modern, warm, refined and fashion-conscious.

The owner experience should prioritize operational information and clarity rather than decorative analytics.

## Important boundaries

* Do not build the complete booking engine yet.

* Do not implement payment integrations yet.

* Do not implement email, WhatsApp or background notifications yet.

* Do not invent appointment durations, prices, deposit amounts or business policies.

* Do not introduce unnecessary third-party services.

* Do not create fake testimonials or business claims.

* Do not treat placeholder information as verified business data.

Use clearly identified placeholders only where needed for the initial interface.

## Completion requirements

At the end of this stage:

1. The application foundation should be coherent and responsive.

2. Both public and owner-facing routes should be present.

3. The visual system should be consistent.

4. The implementation should be structured to support the next stages without unnecessary rewrites.

**Stop after Stage 1. Do not proceed to the remaining product features until instructed.**
